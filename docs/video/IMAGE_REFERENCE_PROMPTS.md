# IMAGE REFERENCE PROMPTS — PetaNadi Video
## Untuk digunakan di: Google Flow Image Mode / Imagen 3 / Google AI Studio
## Semua gambar ini digunakan sebagai referensi visual sebelum generate video di Flow

---

> **Cara generate:**
> 1. Buka Google AI Studio atau Imagen di Flow
> 2. Paste prompt di bawah ke image generation mode
> 3. Generate 3–4 variasi, pilih yang paling sesuai
> 4. Simpan di folder `docs/video/reference-images/`
> 5. Upload ke Flow sebagai ingredient/starting frame

---

## KONSISTENSI WARNA — BRIEF GLOBAL

Sebelum generate tiap gambar, ingat parameter ini:
- **Background dominan:** #0D0D0D sampai #1A1A1A (near-black)
- **Accent color:** #2E7D32 (forest green) — untuk UI glow, node, elemen teknis
- **Warna sekunder:** Putih bersih untuk teks, amber untuk warning, biru gelap untuk air/langit
- **Tone overall:** Desaturated, cinematic, slightly cool cast kecuali scene human (warm praktis)
- **Hindari:** Warna neon, bright cyan, merah terang, background putih

## REF-001 — Jalan Pegunungan Berastagi Subuh (Shot 1A — Opening Hook)

**Digunakan untuk:** Shot pertama video — jalan pegunungan Berastagi sebelum fajar

**Cara dapat:** Generate via Imagen

**PROMPT:**
```
A winding two-lane asphalt road cutting through highland agricultural land in Berastagi, Karo Regency, North Sumatra, Indonesia. Photographed before dawn — the sky is a deep dark blue-black (#0D1117) with the faintest hint of navy-grey at the horizon. Thin wisps of cool mountain fog drift across the road surface at ground level. In the far distance along a curve: a single pair of warm truck headlights (2700K) are visible, cutting through the fog. Both sides of the road: lush highland vegetable farms — chili plants, cabbage crops, terraced green agriculture. Wooden farm structures barely visible in the dark. No streetlights. The scene is lit only by the distant truck headlights and faint blue moonlight catching the edges of leaves and the road surface. Composition: the road occupies the center of frame, curving away into the distance. Camera angle: slightly elevated, looking down the road. Cinematic color grade: deep desaturated blues, green on vegetation edges preserved. Photorealistic. Documentary photography style. No text, no people visible.
```

**Search reference:** "jalan berkelok pegunungan Berastagi" atau "jalan gunung Karo Sumatera Utara subuh" for mood reference

## REF-004 — Multi-Agent Network Graph (Shot 3C — AI Swarm)

**Digunakan untuk:** Visualisasi 6 agen AI yang bekerja paralel

**Cara dapat:** Generate via Imagen

**PROMPT:**
```
A scientific visualization of a multi-agent AI network on a pure black background. Six nodes arranged in a roughly hexagonal pattern — each node is a glowing green sphere (#2E7D32) approximately the same size, connected to multiple other nodes by thin luminous green lines. Each node has a small white label beneath it (the text itself can be illegible — what matters is the structure and visual language). The nodes vary slightly in brightness — two or three are at maximum brightness (suggesting active processing), others are at 60-70% brightness. Traveling light pulses — small bright dots — move along the connection lines in multiple directions simultaneously, suggesting parallel data transmission. The overall composition is centered, with the six nodes distributed evenly. Background: absolute black — no stars, no texture. The only light sources are the nodes and lines themselves. Style: high-end scientific visualization, photorealistic lighting physics, no cartoon elements. The network should feel like a living, breathing computational system — not a static diagram. Aspect ratio 16:9. Ultra high definition.
```

## GOOGLE SEARCH TERMS — Gambar yang Dicari, Bukan Digenerate

### Search 1: Aerial Harbor (Shot 1B)
```
Search terms:
- "pelabuhan belawan dari udara malam" 
- "tanjung priok aerial view night"
- "Indonesian port aerial dusk"

Yang dicari: Foto aerial pelabuhan besar Indonesia saat senja/malam, tampak lampu port, container, kapal. Hindari foto siang hari. Resolusi minimal 1920x1080. License: pilih yang public domain atau berita.

Sumber yang bisa dicoba: Antara Foto, Kompas.com foto, JICA Indonesia reports, Port Authority annual reports.
```

### Search 2: Truck National Road (Shot 2A)
```
Search terms:
- "truk kontainer jalan lintas sumatera"
- "kontainer truck Trans-Sumatera highway"
- "logistik truk jalan nasional Indonesia mendung"

Yang dicari: Truk besar (container atau box truck) di jalan dua arah lurus, langit mendung, lanskap Sumatera atau Jawa. Sisi jalan: perkebunan atau sawah. Waktu: siang mendung atau sore.

Hindari: foto malam, foto di kota, truk yang sangat old-school (tahun 90an feel).
```

### Search 3: Flooded Road (Shot 2B)
```
Search terms:
- "banjir pantura 2024"
- "banjir jalan nasional truk berhenti Indonesia"
- "flood Indonesian national road trucks"

Yang dicari: Banjir setinggi ban truk di jalan nasional Indonesia. Truk berhenti. Air coklat. Langit gelap/mendung. Ini sebaiknya foto berita asli — autentisitas penting.
```
