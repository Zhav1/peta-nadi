## 1. Scope Lock & Non-Overlapping Execution Tracks

To execute Phase 179 safely with zero file-collision across Flash subagents, the phase is partitioned into 4 strict, non-overlapping file-ownership tracks followed by Track 5 (Verification & Acceptance Suite):

### Track A — Honest Fallbacks Purge, H3 Spatial Incident Fusion Engine & News Rubric Gate (D2, D3, D4, D7, D8)
**Owned Files:**
- `backend/app/services/spatial_fusion_engine.py` *(NEW)*
- `backend/app/services/observed_disaster_service.py`
- `backend/app/nlp/news_extractor.py`
- `backend/app/routers/news_router.py`
- `backend/app/services/unified_news_ingestor.py`

**Deliverables:**
1. **Purge Hardcoded Flood/Disaster Baselines (D2)**:
   - Remove `NORTHERN_SUMATRA_FLOOD_BASINS`, `get_northern_sumatra_flood_zones()`, `calculate_disaster_radius_km()`, and `generate_circle_polygon()` from `observed_disaster_service.py`.
   - Never synthesize 15–35 km severity circles. If live floods are empty, return `[]` with honest `source_freshness` metadata (`OK` / `STALE` / `DOWN` + `updated_at` / `age_seconds`).
2. **Evidence-Based Footprints & H3 Res-8 Spatial Fusion Engine (D3, D4)**:
   - Create `backend/app/services/spatial_fusion_engine.py` with a deterministic H3 res-8 (~460 m edge) spatial grid indexer (using `h3` if available or deterministic pure-Python hexagonal axial projection at ~460 m cell size so there is zero C-build fragility on Koyeb).
   - Each signal keeps its real geometry:
     - `bmkg_cap`: official CAP kecamatan/area polygon.
     - `petabencana`: RW/kelurahan polygon if present, or report point with a realistic **300 m (`0.3 km`)** localized polygon buffer (never 15–35 km).
     - `news`: geocoded to local Sumatra kelurahan/kecamatan boundary polygon (or 500 m localized corridor buffer around verified kecamatan/corridor coordinates; ungeocoded articles cannot produce map polygons).
     - `firms`: 375 m VIIRS pixel polygon (`±0.0017°` square).
     - `tomtom`: actual slow road `LineString` buffered by 250 m.
   - **Fusion Rules**:
     - Signals of compatible hazard type overlapping in H3 res-8 cells (or within 1-ring H3 neighbors) within their active TTL window merge into **ONE unified incident**.
     - Footprint = geometric union/envelope of corroborated source geometries only (no overlapping duplicate circles stacked on the same spot). Isolated non-overlapping signals stay separate incidents.
     - **Deterministic Authority Tiers (D4)**:
       - `CONFIRMED`: $\ge 2$ independent sources where $\ge 1$ is physical/official (`bmkg_cap`, `petabencana`, `firms`, `tomtom`, `gdacs`).
       - `WATCH`: 1 credible source (rendered with dashed outline on map).
       - Everything else: excluded from map zones.
     - **Hazard TTLs**: Flash flood / weather nowcast = 6h; traffic jam = 30m; road/bridge infrastructure collapse = 48h.
3. **News Rubric & LLM Hold-Back (D4, D7, D8)**:
   - In `news_extractor.py`:
     - Enforce the 5-category logistics disruption rubric (D7): (1) natural hazards affecting movement, (2) road disruptions/closures/accidents/toll issues, (3) port/sea congestion or ferry suspension, (4) BBM/fuel shortages & SPBU truck queues, (5) demonstrations/blockades on logistics routes. Explicitly reject routine CSR/training/ceremonial news (e.g., PLN motor listrik training, Kapolres helm SNI distribution).
     - Drop Gate-2 `mimo-v2.6-pro` (`run_gate2_async_verification` / `_call_mimo` becomes a no-op stub returning Gate-1 result so callers don't break).
     - **Remove regex heuristic fallback on LLM failure** (lines 2154–2160, 2249–2252): if `deepseek-v4.1-flash` fails or times out, mark the article `zone_tier = "HELD_BACK_LLM_UNAVAILABLE"`, `is_disruption = False` — never pass routine news through via regex heuristics.
     - Keep URL/title MD5 hash caching + Supabase `schema_version` deduplication so each article is extracted at most once.
   - In `news_router.py`:
     - Remove the `regional` display leak (`is_regional_context` must return `False`; `classify_relevance_tier` only returns `'crisis'` for validated/watch disruption items or `'dropped'` for everything else).
