# Plan 62-01: Visual UI Slop Eradication and Logistics UX Humanization

## Goal
Eliminate visual AI-slop artifacts (crammed neon badges, detached floating controls, text truncation, and artificial monospace styling) and sanitize all UX copywriting across PreHub dashboard surfaces using the `impeccable` design standard and `humanizer` Indonesian logistics operational terminology.

## Proposed Changes

### 1. Map Controls, Popovers & Floating HUD (`frontend/components/dashboard/DashboardClient.tsx`)
- **Map Legend**: Replace `LEGENDA RUTE` / `Legenda Peta & Keputusan` with `Panduan Simbol & Rute`. Anchor popover cleanly within viewport bounds to eliminate bottom clipping. Remove diffuse neon border glows in favor of crisp `border-slate-800` on elevated `bg-slate-900/95`.
- **Layer Filter Popover**: Replace `FILTER VISUALISASI LAYER` with `Pilihan Lapisan Peta`. Adjust positioning and margin to eliminate left-edge viewport clipping.
- **Header & News Section**: Replace `INTELIJEN BERITA RESMI` with `Warta & Laporan Lapangan`.
- **Hazard Terminology**: Replace `Zona Radius Bahaya (Shockwave)` with `Radius Area Terdampak Bencana`.
- **Guest Banner**: Replace `Mode Evaluasi Tamu (Belum Terautentikasi)` with `Akses Tamu (Mode Pratinjau)`.

### 2. Crisis Card Header & Status Unification (`frontend/components/dashboard/CrisisSidebar.tsx`)
- **Badge Consolidation**: Eliminate badge cramming (`[KRITIS/TINGGI]`, `[91% Keyakinan]`, `[SIMULASI FIXTURE]`).
- Drop the synthetic `SIMULASI FIXTURE` pill entirely.
- Render a unified, calm status line: primary severity tag (`KRITIS`) accompanied by subtle, clear validation metadata (`Tingkat Validasi: 91%`).

### 3. Operator Review Drawer Clean-Up (`frontend/components/dashboard/HitlDecisionDrawer.tsx`)
- **Header**: Replace `HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP REVIEW` with `Persetujuan Rute Operator`.
- **Protocol Tag**: Replace `GLOBOT PROTOCOL` with `Protokol Validasi Otomatis`.
- **Vehicle Selection Pills**: Provide sufficient `min-w` and responsive wrapping to prevent truncation (eliminates `[N...`, `[N...`).
- **Evidence Citation**: Replace `EVIDENCE CITATION (VERIFIED OSINT)` with `Sumber Laporan & Bukti Terverifikasi`.
- **Cost-Benefit Matrix**: Replace `COST-BENEFIT HEDGING MATRIX` with `Analisis Risiko & Biaya Pengalihan`.
- **Metric Cards**:
  - Replace `POTENSI SUSUT / SPOILAGE` with `Risiko Kerusakan Muatan`.
  - Replace `BIAYA DETOUR (+SOLAR/TOL)` with `Tambahan Operasional (BBM & Tol)`.
  - Format commodity data: convert raw snake_case string `bawang_merah (10 Ton)` into human title case `Bawang Merah (10 Ton)`.
- **Action Buttons**: Replace `SETUJUI DETOUR` / `TAHAN BUFFER` with `Setujui Pengalihan Rute` / `Tahan di Titik Aman`.

### 4. Mitigation & Economics Tab Sanity (`frontend/components/dashboard/MitigationTab.tsx`, `frontend/components/dashboard/EconomicTab.tsx`)
- Replace `Konsensus Sistem & Keyakinan` with `Validasi Terpadu Multi-Sumber`.
- Replace `Regim Pasar: Waspada` with `Status Pasokan: Waspada`.
- Replace `Dampak Fisik & Rantai Ekonomi` with `Gangguan Jalur & Dampak Pasokan`.
- Replace `Rincian Leg Logistik Multi-Moda` with `Tahapan Rute Perjalanan Armada`.
- Replace `Horizon proyeksi belum tersedia` with `Rentang estimasi waktu belum tersedia`.
- Standardize descriptive text and headings to `font-sans`, reserving `font-mono` strictly for numeric quantities and coordinate codes.

### 5. Interactive Demo Stepper Copy (`frontend/components/demo/GuidedDemoPanel.tsx`)
- Replace developer prompt syntax `(Klik 1: Start, Klik 2: End)` with natural guidance: `Klik titik asal, lalu titik tujuan pada peta`.
- Replace `Horizon Proyeksi` with `Periode Estimasi Dampak`.
- Sanitize stage headers and descriptions to sound like professional operations guidance.

### 6. Analytics Transmission Sequence & Simulation Refinement (`frontend/components/dashboard/AnalyticsSection.tsx`, `frontend/components/dashboard/SimulationSection.tsx`)
- **Analytics Causal Chain**: Replace `RANTAI KAUSALITAS DAMPAK` with `Alur Penjalaran Dampak Gangguan`.
- Redesign the cramped 4-column card row into a responsive sequence/timeline that allows full text rendering without ellipsis truncation (`Gerba...`, `Bottl...`, `Komodit...`).
- Replace `Delta Bawang: -9.1%` with `Perubahan Harga Bawang: -9.1%`.
- **Simulation**: Replace `SIMULATOR MAKRO (B2G): KALKULASI LOKAL` with `Simulasi Kebijakan Distribusi Komoditas`.
- Replace `Peta 4D` and `commit ke koridor` with `Peta Operasional` and `terapkan ke rute armada`.
