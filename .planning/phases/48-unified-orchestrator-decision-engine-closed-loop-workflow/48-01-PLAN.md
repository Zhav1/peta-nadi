## 1. Objective
Restructure the LangGraph `StateGraph` in [graph.py](file:///d:/College/Pidi.id/agents/graph.py) from the flawed parallel fan-out into a 4-stage pipeline:
1. **Stage 1 (Ingestion):** `data_collection`
