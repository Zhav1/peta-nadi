# Plan 44-01: Backend Choke-Point Synchronization, Spoilage Hedging Solver & Digital Compliance Engine

**Phase:** 44  
**Plan:** 44-01  
**Goal:** Implement FastAPI backend services and REST endpoints for 20+ Pan-Sumatra transport choke-points (7 sea/ferry ports + 11 mountain/toll conjunctions), a dynamic operational spoilage hedging solver with real BPJT toll tariffs and Pertamina fuel modulation, and a digital compliance inspection engine (BKHIT quarantine hard blocks and MST axle-load warnings).

---

## 1. Requirements & Invariants

- **FR-17.1**: Pan-Sumatra Transport Choke-Point & Port Queue Synchronizer (`GET /api/v1/intermodal/chokepoints`): Monitor 20+ strategic Sumatra gateways (7 sea/ferry ports and 11 mountain/toll bottlenecks), calculating dwelling times, vessel/truck queues, and status (`NORMAL`, `CONGESTED`, `RESTRICTED`, `BLOCKED`).
- **FR-17.2**: Automated Intermodal Delay Multiplier: Compute $M_{\text{intermodal}} = 1.0 + 0.15 \times N_{\text{queue}} \times \text{SeverityFactor}$ clamped strictly to $[1.0, 3.5]$.
- **FR-18.1**: Dynamic Spoilage Hedging Cost Matrix Solver (`POST /api/v1/intermodal/hedging/solve`): Closed-form evaluation of $\text{Continue}$ vs $\text{Reroute}$ vs $\text{Hold}$ factoring:
  - 4-Tier Perishability Decay Model: Ultra-Perishable ($\delta = 0.025/\text{hr}$), Cold-Chain ($\delta = 0.015/\text{hr}$ + IDR 45,000/hr genset), Semi-Perishable ($\delta = 0.008/\text{hr}$), Non-Perishable ($\delta = 0.0005/\text{hr}$).
  - Real BPJT Toll Tariffs by vehicle classification (Golongan II–V) across 7 major Sumatra toll sections.
  - Real Pertamina benchmark fuel rates (Biosolar IDR 6,800/L, Dexlite IDR 14,550/L, Pertamina Dex IDR 15,100/L) modulated dynamically by Market Regime inflation shock.
  - Dynamic PIHPS spot cargo valuation ($\text{CargoValue} = \text{Tonnage} \times \text{SpotPrice}$).
- **FR-19.1**: Digital Cargo Manifest & Quarantine Compliance Inspector (`POST /api/v1/intermodal/compliance/verify`):
  - Hard Block on missing BKHIT agricultural quarantine certificates for inter-island routes.
  - Tactical Warning & Reroute Advisory for heavy vehicles (>8 Ton) traversing Class III collector/mountain roads.
  - Surat Jalan verification matching plate, driver, commodity, and manifest hash.
- **NFR-11.2**: Deterministic, zero-boasting, sub-millisecond execution for all mathematical solver routines.

## 2. Technical Scope & Implementation Steps

### Task 1: Intermodal Schemas (`backend/app/schemas/intermodal.py`)
- Define Pydantic v2 schemas:
  - `ChokePointItem`: `id`, `name`, `type` (`SEAPORT` | `FERRY_TERMINAL` | `MOUNTAIN_PASS` | `TOLL_HIGHWAY_JUNCTION` | `FREIGHT_CORRIDOR` | `HIGHWAY_BOTTLENECK`), `coords: List[float]`, `province`, `status` (`NORMAL` | `CONGESTED` | `RESTRICTED` | `BLOCKED`), `dwelling_time_hours`, `queue_count`, `intermodal_delay_multiplier`, `hazard_type`, `updated_at`.
  - `ChokePointsListResponse`: `items: List[ChokePointItem]`, `total: int`, `congested_count: int`.
  - `HedgingSolveRequest`: `vehicle_id: str`, `commodity: str`, `cargo_tonnage: float`, `origin: str`, `destination: str`, `vehicle_golongan: str` (default `"GOL_II"`), `fuel_type: str` (default `"biosolar"`), `p_disruption: float`, `disruption_delay_hours: float`, `detour_distance_km: float`, `detour_time_hours: float`, `toll_segments: Optional[List[str]]`, `hold_wait_hours: float`.
  - `PolicyBreakdown`: `cost_idr: float`, `breakdown: Dict[str, float]`, `explanation: str`, `risk_level: str`.
  - `HedgingSolveResponse`: `vehicle_id`, `commodity`, `perishability_tier`, `cargo_value_idr`, `spoilage_loss_idr`, `continue_policy: PolicyBreakdown`, `reroute_policy: PolicyBreakdown`, `hold_policy: PolicyBreakdown`, `optimal_policy: str`, `net_savings_idr: float`, `recommendation_reason: str`.
  - `ComplianceVerifyRequest`: `vehicle_id: str`, `origin: str`, `destination: str`, `traversed_roads: List[str]`, `vehicle_gross_weight_ton: float`, `commodity: str`, `driver_phone: Optional[str]`, `license_plate: Optional[str]`, `has_bkhit_cert: bool`, `bkhit_cert_id: Optional[str]`, `manifest_hash: Optional[str]`.
  - `ComplianceCheckDetail`: `category: str`, `status` (`PASSED` | `WARNING` | `HARD_BLOCK`), `title: str`, `detail: str`, `remedy_action: Optional[str]`.
  - `ComplianceVerifyResponse`: `overall_status` (`PASSED` | `WARNING` | `HARD_BLOCK`), `can_dispatch: bool`, `requires_override: bool`, `checks: List[ComplianceCheckDetail]`, `timestamp: str`.

### Task 2: 20+ Pan-Sumatra Choke-Points & Terminal Queuing (`backend/app/services/intermodal_sync_service.py`)
- Register the 18+ authoritative Sumatra transport hubs and bottlenecks:
  - 7 Sea/Ferry Terminals: Pelabuhan Belawan, Pelabuhan Bakauheni, Pelabuhan Dumai, Pelabuhan Teluk Bayur, Pelabuhan Panjang, Pelabuhan Sibolga, Pelabuhan Kuala Tanjung.
  - 11 Mountain Passes & Conjunction Bottlenecks: Tanjakan Sitinjau Lauik, Kelok 9 Payakumbuh, Jalur Lingkar Malalak, Batu Lubang Tarutung-Sibolga, Interchange Tebing Tinggi, Simpang Duri - Kandis, Bottleneck Betung - Palembang, Interchange Terbanggi Besar, Simpang Muara Tembesi, Lintas Curup - Kepahiang, Lintas Gunung Seulawah.
- Dynamic queue and status calculation:
  - Fuse TomTom traffic congestion and BMKG spatial weather alerts on the conjunction coordinates.
  - Compute dwelling times ($T_{\text{dwell}}$) and delay multiplier:
    $$M_{\text{intermodal}} = 1.0 + 0.15 \times N_{\text{queue}} \times \text{SeverityWeight}$$
    clamped to $[1.0, 3.5]$.
- Methods:
  - `get_all_chokepoints() -> List[Dict[str, Any]]`
  - `get_chokepoint_by_id(chokepoint_id: str) -> Optional[Dict[str, Any]]`
  - `calculate_route_intermodal_delay(route_waypoints: List[List[float]]) -> float`

### Task 3: Spoilage Hedging Solver with BPJT Toll & Pertamina Fuel (`backend/app/services/spoilage_hedging_service.py`)
- Implement `BPJT_SUMATRA_TOLL_SEGMENTS` with exact official tariffs for Golongan I through V:
  - `BAKAUHENI_TERBANGGI`, `TERBANGGI_KAYUAGUNG`, `KAYUAGUNG_PALEMBANG`, `PEKANBARU_DUMAI`, `MEDAN_TEBINGTINGGI`, `BELMERA`, `SIGLI_BANDAACEH`.
- Implement `PERTAMINA_FUEL_BASE_RATES`:
  - `biosolar`: IDR 6,800/L, `dexlite`: IDR 14,550/L, `pertamina_dex`: IDR 15,100/L.
- Dynamic fuel inflation modulation:
  - Query Market Regime from `news_router.py` / `news_aggregator.py` to modulate base fuel rates under active logistics shocks.
- 4-Tier Perishability Model:
  - Categorize commodities (Cabai, Tomat $\to$ Ultra-Perishable $\delta = 0.025$; Daging, Ikan $\to$ Cold-Chain $\delta = 0.015$; Bawang, Kentang $\to$ Semi-Perishable $\delta = 0.008$; Beras, Minyak $\to$ Dry Bulk $\delta = 0.0005$).
  - Spot price integration with fallback to `commodity_router.py` base price table.
- Solver method:
  - `solve_hedging_matrix(req: HedgingSolveRequest) -> HedgingSolveResponse`
  - Calculate `Cost(Continue)`, `Cost(Reroute)`, `Cost(Hold)`, identify minimum monetary loss, and format net savings.

### Task 4: Digital Compliance Inspector Engine (`backend/app/services/compliance_service.py`)
- Implement inspection rules:
  1. **BKHIT Inter-Island Quarantine Rule**:
     - Check if route traverses Sunda Strait (Bakauheni $\leftrightarrow$ Merak) or Malacca Strait (Belawan / Dumai $\leftrightarrow$ Riau Islands).
     - If inter-island and `has_bkhit_cert` is `False`, flag `HARD_BLOCK` (`can_dispatch: False`).
  2. **MST Axle-Load Rule**:
     - Check traversed road classes: if any segment is `CLASS_III` (e.g., Malalak, Dairi, secondary ring roads) and `vehicle_gross_weight_ton > 8.0`, flag `TACTICAL_WARNING` with reroute advisory (`requires_override: True`).
  3. **Surat Jalan / Manifest Integrity**:
     - Check driver contact, vehicle plate, and verify `manifest_hash`.
- Method:
  - `verify_compliance(req: ComplianceVerifyRequest) -> ComplianceVerifyResponse`

### Task 5: REST Router & Main App Registration (`backend/app/routers/intermodal_router.py`)
- Mount endpoints with prefix `/api/v1/intermodal`:
  - `GET /chokepoints` — Return all 18+ Pan-Sumatra choke-points and terminal statuses.
  - `GET /chokepoints/{chokepoint_id}` — Return details for a single gateway.
  - `POST /hedging/solve` — Calculate Spoilage Hedging Cost Matrix.
  - `POST /compliance/verify` — Inspect Surat Jalan, BKHIT, and MST compliance.
  - `GET /toll-tariffs` — Retrieve the official BPJT Sumatra toll tariff tables.
- Register `intermodal_router` in `backend/app/main.py`.
