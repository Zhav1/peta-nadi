## Executive Summary

Phase 38 establishes the complete empirical feedback loop for PreHub. Before Phase 38, recommendations generated by the multi-agent swarm terminated in a single, unvaried approval record without structured operator feedback, ground-truth field outcomes, or variance evaluation.

With Phase 38:
1. **Operator Decision Tracing (FR-12.1, FR-12.2):** Full taxonomy of actions (ACCEPT, REJECT, OVERRIDE) and tactical execution maneuvers (REROUTE, HOLD, CONTINUE) with mandatory rationale validation.
2. **Local ACID Storage Fallback (NFR-8):** Thread-safe SQLite persistence (ackend/data/prehub_local.db) ensures zero decision trace or outcome data loss during network partition or database maintenance.
3. **Ground-Truth Outcome Recording (FR-12.4):** Dual-horizon field verification (T+12h and T+24h) captures observed delay hours and actual market price spikes from official and ground sources.
4. **Prediction vs. Outcome Variance & Recalibration (FR-12.3, NFR-9):** Closed-loop evaluation computes delay and price error deltas and generates damped (eta = 0.05) sensor weight recalibration advisories.
5. **Interactive Tactical UI:** Operators have direct access to granular command buttons (REROUTE, HOLD, OVERRIDE) within MitigationTab.tsx.
